What Public Records Revealed

104 cameras purchased secretly with COVID relief funds. The original contract was for 31 cameras. An additional 104 were bought with federal ARPA money — with no public hearing or dedicated council vote on the expansion.
An officer searched plates 2 hours after their shift ended with no case, no incident, and no documented reason. The city refuses to say whether any investigation followed.
A third of searched plates couldn't be justified. Internal audits show officers routinely enter "test" or meaningless codes instead of case numbers. Auditors flagged searches they couldn't verify — but no consequences followed.
Your data is shared with 6 law enforcement agencies through indefinite agreements with no expiration date, no public reporting, and no external oversight.
